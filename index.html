Read this before you spend it

Every dollar I keep goes to work for me, every day, for decades.

  • TimeTime is the biggest lever. The money barely moves for the first ten years. After that the curve bends up hard, and the last decade earns more than the first two combined.
  • FreedomSavings buy options: turning down a bad job, taking time for school, covering an emergency without debt.
  • RulesStart early, keep adding, and don't touch it. Pulling money out early flattens the hockey stick before it ever bends.
